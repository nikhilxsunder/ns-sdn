## Provenance

The package is the software artifact of research conducted under the ICSRI
Fellows Program. Authorship of the code is confined to the fellowship for
that reason; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for what that means in
practice and for the ways to help that do not involve writing code.
